The King's Showdown Rules

  • The King's Qualification: The King must hold Queen-high or better (Q-6-4 or higher) to qualify.
  • The King Does Not Qualify: Ante wager pays 1.4×. Play wager pushes (returned 1.0×).
  • The King Qualifies & Player Wins: Ante wager pays 1:1, Play wager pays 1:1 (Total return: 2.0×).
  • Royal Push / Tie: If Player and The King hold identical hands and kickers, all wagers push (1.0× returned).
  • The King Prevails: Ante and Play wagers are surrendered (0×). Any Ante Bonus earned is still awarded.
  • Free Games: Table card game — there are no free spins or feature re-triggers.

3-Card Poker Hierarchy

Ranked from highest to lowest. In 3-card poker, Straight outranks Flush!

1. Mini Royal Flush
A♠ K♠ Q♠
Ace, King, Queen of the same suit. The supreme royal hand.
2. Straight Flush
9♥ 8♥ 7♥
Three consecutive cards of the exact same suit.
3. Three of a Kind
8♠ 8♦ 8♣
Three cards of matching rank.
4. Straight
J♦ 10♠ 9♣
Three cards of sequential rank (A-2-3 is low straight).
5. Flush
K♦ 9♦ 2♦
Three non-consecutive cards of matching suit.
6. Pair
10♠ 10♥ 4♣
Two cards of matching rank with one kicker.
7. High Card
A♣ J♦ 6♠
Highest single card held when no combinations form.
