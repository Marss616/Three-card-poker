Main Game Payouts

  • Dealer Qualification: Dealer must have Queen-high or better (Q-6-4 or higher) to qualify.
  • Dealer Does Not Qualify: Ante bet pays 1.4×. Play bet pushes (returned 1.0×).
  • Dealer Qualifies & Player Beats Dealer: Ante bet pays 1:1, Play bet pays 1:1 (Total return: 2.0×).
  • Tie / Push: If Player and Dealer have identical hand rankings and kickers, all wagers push (1.0× returned).
  • Dealer Beats Player: Ante and Play wagers are lost (0×). Any Ante Bonus earned is still awarded.
  • Free Games: Table card game — there are no free spins or feature re-triggers.

3-Card Poker Hierarchy

Ranked from highest to lowest. In 3-card poker, Straight ranks higher than Flush!

1. Mini Royal Flush
A♠ K♠ Q♠
Ace, King, Queen of the same suit.
2. Straight Flush
9♥ 8♥ 7♥
Three cards in consecutive rank of the same suit.
3. Three of a Kind
8♠ 8♦ 8♣
Three cards of the exact same rank.
4. Straight
J♦ 10♠ 9♣
Three consecutive cards of mixed suits (A-2-3 is lowest straight).
5. Flush
K♦ 9♦ 2♦
Three non-consecutive cards of the same suit.
6. Pair
10♠ 10♥ 4♣
Two cards of matching rank with one kicker.
7. High Card
A♣ J♦ 6♠
Highest single card held when no matching combination exists.

User Interaction Guide

  • Placing Wagers: Click or tap any chip value at the bottom to adjust your Ante wager. Use ½ or 2× for quick bet halving or doubling.
  • Starting a Round: Click the DEAL button or press the Space bar on your keyboard.
  • Play Bet: An equal Play bet is automatically matched to your Ante to proceed directly to showdown.
  • Auto Play: Click ⚡ Auto to configure automated rounds. A confirmation dialog will appear before auto-play begins. You can cancel at any time with the STOP AUTO button.
  • Audio Settings: Toggle sound effects on or off using the 🔊/🔇 icon in the header. Your preference is automatically saved.
  • Dismissing Results: When a round completes, tap anywhere on the screen to clear the result banner and deal again.
  • Replays: Round replays can be viewed via authorized replay links with full transparency.
